Data Protection & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

313 Tech Solutions ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and security of personal and commercial information entrusted to us by clients, website visitors, and partners worldwide. This policy outlines our principles regarding data collection, processing, storage, and cross-border transfers.

2. Information We Collect

We process information strictly necessary for commercial engagement, technical evaluation, and service delivery:

  • Contact & Discovery Data: Name, business email, telephone number, organization name, role, and project specifications provided when requesting consultations or proposals.
  • Technical & Analytical Data: IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and page interaction metrics collected via telemetry and server access logs to optimize site performance and security.
  • Client Project Information: Technical documentation, architectural blueprints, and requirements exchanged under mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

3. Legal Basis & Use of Information

We process data under lawful bases including performance of contracts, legitimate commercial interests, legal obligations, and explicit user consent. Data is utilized solely to:

  • Respond to business inquiries and provide technical proposals.
  • Fulfill software development, engineering, and technology consulting agreements.
  • Ensure the security, uptime, and integrity of our systems and services.
  • Comply with applicable legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

We never sell, rent, or trade client or prospect information to third-party advertisers.

4. International Data Transfers & Compliance

As an international technology partner collaborating with clients across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, European Union, Australia, and other global regions, we ensure appropriate safeguards for cross-border data transfers. We implement Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), robust role-based encryption (AES-256 in transit and at rest), and ISO-aligned engineering hygiene to adhere to the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain client communications and engagement data only as long as necessary to fulfill project requirements, warranty commitments, and legal recordkeeping standards. Technical logs are systematically purged after 90 days. All engineering repositories and confidential assets are governed by strict multi-factor authentication (MFA) and least-privilege access controls.
